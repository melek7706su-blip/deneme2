Mars navigation decision support

Your intelligent companion for safe exploration on Mars.

MarsPath Pro is an integrated navigation system designed to combine astronauts’ location, environmental, and mission data to identify safe routes and support sound decisions when hazards arise.

Explore the project Open the PDF presentation

About the project

Communication delays with Earth, dust storms, extreme temperature changes, and unknown terrain make decision-making difficult for astronauts on Mars. MarsPath Pro helps manage these challenges by bringing satellite imagery, topographic information, and data from past scientific missions together in a single interface.

01

Satellite imagery

Helps assess the Martian surface and identify traversable areas.

02

Topographic data

Supports route assessment by taking slopes, rocks, and craters into account.

03

Scientific data

Combines measurements from past missions and scientifically significant areas on the map.

Key benefits

Safe routes

Suggests safer walking routes for astronauts based on terrain conditions.

Scientific exploration

Highlights research targets such as ancient ice reserves and valuable mineral areas.

Dynamic rerouting

Supports the recommendation of an alternative route when a new hazard appears.

Safety and emergency response

The system uses a three-level warning approach by assessing astronauts’ condition and environmental risks.

Green · Normal

Continue on route

Life support, connectivity, and navigation information are within expected ranges.

Yellow · Caution

Slow down

A decline is detected in oxygen, battery, temperature, or connectivity conditions.

Red · Critical

Stop the mission

In the event of serious risk, the mission is stopped and moving to a safe shelter is recommended.
